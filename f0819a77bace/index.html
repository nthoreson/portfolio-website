How It Works

Assign. Complete. Review.

You have been running a version of this already, since the Notifications Center lets you push embedded videos and require acknowledgment. Academy takes the same instinct and gives it structure, so content sits in courses, gets assigned by role or by station, and gets tracked over time rather than confirmed once and forgotten.

STEP 01

Assign

Pick a module in the Admin Console and send it to a role, a station, or the full roster, with a due date if you need one.

STEP 02

Complete

Crews open Academy inside the OneDose app, where modules run short, progress saves between shifts, and a quiz confirms the workflow stuck.

STEP 03

Review

Completion and quiz results land in your admin reporting alongside the analytics you already read.

Launch Catalog

Modules mapped to the tools you already own.

Content splits into two tracks so console material stays off a medic's phone. Every module is short by design and ends with a quiz tied to the workflow rather than the terminology, because what matters is whether someone can run the tool on a call, not whether they can name it.

Provider Track

  • PRV 101Dosing and Cross-Check
    Weight, age, and length based dosing, dose range validation, and clinical override without breaking the workflow.
  • PRV 102eMACC Verification
    Confirming medication, concentration, and calculated dose in the field with a single provider.
  • PRV 103CPR Assist
    Haptic compression timing, time stamped intervention logging, and how it maps to your protocols.
  • PRV 104Checklists and Scoring
    Working through protocol driven pathways and running GCS and trauma scores without manual math.
  • PRV 105Live Patient Record and Handoff
    Multi provider documentation on one encounter and secure web link sharing at the hospital.
  • PRV 106AI Assistant
    Asking protocol questions, documenting by voice, and what the assistant will and will not answer.
  • PRV 107OneWeight
    For gurney equipped agencies. Capturing patient weight and carrying it into dosing.

Administrator Track

  • ADM 201Admin Console Fundamentals
    Medications, user tiers, permissions, and agency level customization.
  • ADM 202Protocol Publishing and Version Control
    Pushing updates instantly, holding to your approval workflow, and pulling historic versions for audit.
  • ADM 203Analytics and QA/QI
    Reading adoption and usage data, and turning it into a training plan you can defend.
  • ADM 204ePCR Integration
    Agency specific. Covers your platform, whether that is ImageTrend, ESO, First Due, AmbuPro, EPR Fireworks, Sansio HealthEMS, or Zoi.

Visibility

See where the gaps are.

Academy reporting shows completion by role and by station, along with which features are unused after training, which tells you where the next training should focus. Read it the same way you read Administrative Analytics, as a map of knowledge gaps rather than a scorecard. It also leaves you a record, so when a protocol changes or a tool goes live, you can show your medical director and your QA committee that the change reached the field.

Assumptions

Built from the public OneDose site without access to internal launch details. Each item below would change the page if it turns out differently.

  1. Academy is included at every tier at no added cost, covering Essentials and Professional rather than only Agency Plus and Agency Premium. The zero cost claim on this page depends on it.
  2. Launch content is authored by OneDose, with agency authored content as a later phase. If agencies can upload their own material at launch, this becomes a page about onboarding rather than product training and gets rebuilt.
  3. Completion and quiz data appear in the existing Admin Console alongside Administrative Analytics rather than in a separate system.
  4. Scope is OneDose product training. No CE credit is offered or implied.
  5. Module topics come from published platform features, but the catalog itself is proposed rather than confirmed. The two track split, the course codes, the count of eleven modules, and the count of sixteen capabilities are all estimates.
  6. Academy behavior described here is assumed from comparable platforms, including module length, saved progress, due dates, assignment by role or station, and continued access to completed modules.
